# Style

The store's voice: modern Saudi commercial Arabic — natural spoken sensibility, polished written execution. It sits between formal MSA and everyday conversation.

It is not: generic formal MSA, literal translation from English, exaggerated dialect, chat language, or generic Gulf marketing.

## Voice

**واضح، ذكي، قريب، واثق، عملي** — a knowledgeable local retailer helping the customer decide.

Not: **متكلف، متفلسف، متحمس زيادة، متصنع لغة شبابية**.

- Speak to the customer, not like the customer. Familiar, never sloppy.
- Confidence comes from clarity and useful information, not exaggeration.
- Authenticity comes from natural phrasing, not dialect density. A listing can be strongly Saudi-facing in neutral Arabic.
- The reader should encounter the product first, not the writing technique.

## Tone

The voice stays constant; the temperature follows the product. Elegant for perfume, energetic for gaming, appetizing for food, reassuring for a medical booking, calm and practical for household goods. These all remain the same store.

## Examples

Desired:

> إذا تبي قهوة سهلة وسريعة التحضير، هذا الخيار مناسب لروتينك اليومي. حجمها عملي وتقدر تاخذها معك للدوام أو السفر.

Too formal:

> يُعد هذا المنتج خيارًا مناسبًا للأفراد الباحثين عن وسيلة عملية وفعالة تلبي متطلبات الاستخدام اليومي.

Too casual:

> هذا رهيب مرّة! صدق ما راح تندم إذا أخذته، وبيصير معك بكل مكان.

Generic / AI-like:

> يقدم هذا المنتج تجربة استثنائية تجمع بين الجودة العالية والأداء المتميز والتصميم العصري لتلبية مختلف احتياجاتك.

Too salesy:

> لا تفوت الفرصة! هذا المنتج لازم يكون عندك الآن، وأكيد راح يكون أفضل اختيار لك!

Artificially Saudi:

> يا بعد حيي، هذا المنتج خرافي وبيخليك مبسوط مرّة!

## Writing

- Direct: reach the useful point without introduction. No generic statement about quality, innovation, or excellence before the product itself.
- Every sentence carries product information, meaningful explanation, or justified persuasion. No sentence exists only to sound impressive.
- Easy to read on a screen. Simple language when it communicates accurately; complexity only from the product.
- No repetition to add length.
- No formula: structure follows the product and its customer decisions. Identity → what matters → practical meaning → supporting details is a common path, never a mandatory one.
- Lists when scanning distinct items helps (contents, key specs, options). Headings only in longer copy. Emphasis sparingly.
- Connect a feature to its practical meaning only when the connection is directly supported by the facts. When uncertain, state the feature itself.
- A closing is optional; add one only when it carries real value.

## Terminology and translation

Arabic is primary. English is fine when Saudi customers genuinely use the term or it is the recognized platform, product, or standard name. Never translate word-for-word from source material — translate meaning so the copy reads as Arabic written for the customer. Specific term choices live in `VOCABULARY.md`.
