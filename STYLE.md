# Style

## Purpose

This file defines the voice, tone, naturalness, and overall linguistic character of the store's Saudi-facing product copy.

The target is **modern Saudi commercial Arabic**: Arabic written for Saudi customers in a contemporary, natural, professionally edited way.

The copy should feel like it was written by a capable Saudi e-commerce marketer who understands how people in Saudi Arabia actually read and talk about products online.

This file defines the desired _character_ of the writing.

Detailed writing rules, vocabulary, prohibited expressions, workflow, product-data requirements, and QA tests belong in their respective project files.

---

## Target Language

### Modern Saudi Commercial Arabic

The target is:

> **لغة عربية سعودية معاصرة، طبيعية وقريبة، لكن مكتوبة باحتراف.**

It sits between formal written Arabic and everyday Saudi conversation.

It is **not**:

- generic formal MSA;
- literal Arabic translated from English;
- exaggerated Saudi dialect;
- chat language;
- social-media slang;
- generic "Gulf" marketing language.

The goal is written language that feels **native to the Saudi commercial context**, not language that performs "Saudi-ness."

### Natural Saudi Language

Saudi authenticity should come primarily from:

- natural phrasing;
- familiar ways of explaining products;
- appropriate levels of directness;
- normal Saudi commercial expectations;
- language that does not feel translated or artificially localized.

It does **not** mean deliberately adding dialect words to every sentence.

Words such as:

> "وش"، "تبي"، "مرّة"

may sometimes be completely natural, but they are examples of possible language choices—not requirements and not a definition of Saudi writing.

A description can be strongly Saudi-facing while using relatively neutral Arabic.

### What "Natural" Means

The reader should not feel that the writer is trying to sound Saudi.

The language should feel effortless.

Avoid language that feels:

- translated from English;
- artificially formal;
- overly literary;
- mechanically enthusiastic;
- stuffed with slang;
- copied from generic advertising;
- generated from a reusable marketing formula.

The reader should encounter the product first, not the writing technique.

## Brand Voice

### Core Personality

The default personality is:

**واضح، ذكي، قريب، واثق، وعملي.**

The store should sound like a knowledgeable local retailer helping the customer make a good decision.

It should not sound:

**متفلسف، متكلف، متحمس زيادة، أو متصنع لغة شبابية.**

### Professionalism

Professional does not mean corporate.

The copy should feel polished and trustworthy without sounding like a government announcement, formal report, or corporate brochure.

Compare the character of:

> "يُعد هذا المنتج خيارًا مناسبًا للأفراد الباحثين عن وسيلة عملية وفعالة تلبي متطلبات الاستخدام اليومي."

with:

> "إذا تبي خيار عملي للاستخدام اليومي، هذا المنتج يعطيك تجربة أسهل بدون تعقيد."

The second is closer to the intended character because it speaks naturally to the customer rather than performing formality.

The exact wording is not a template. These examples demonstrate the intended voice.

### Familiarity

The language should feel close to the customer without pretending to be a conversation between friends.

The store speaks **to** the customer, not **like** the customer.

Natural familiarity is good.

Forced slang is not.

### Confidence

The voice should be confident when the available product information supports confidence.

Confidence should come from clarity and useful information, not from exaggeration.

---

## Tone

### Default Tone

The default tone is:

**ودود + واضح + واثق + عملي.**

The copy should make the product easier to understand and help the customer decide whether it suits them.

It should not behave like an advertisement shouting for attention in every sentence.

### Emotional Range

The voice can become:

- warm;
- premium;
- energetic;
- reassuring;
- practical;
- convenient;
- exciting;

when the product warrants it.

The **voice stays consistent while the emotional temperature changes**.

A perfume may feel elegant.

A gaming product may feel energetic.

A food product may feel appetizing.

A medical booking may feel reassuring.

A practical household product may feel calm and useful.

These should still feel like the same store.

### Product-Context Variation

Do not apply one emotional template to every product.

The style should adapt to the customer's expectations of the category while preserving the same underlying personality.

## Linguistic Character

### Spoken-vs-Written Balance

The language should be easy to read aloud naturally but still feel like professional written commerce.

It should not sound like:

- a newspaper;
- a legal document;
- an academic paper;
- a government circular;
- an unedited WhatsApp message.

The desired position is:

> **natural spoken sensibility + polished written execution.**

### Rhythm and Readability

Good copy has an easy rhythm.

Simple product information should not become complicated because of long sentences, unnecessary clauses, or formal constructions.

The writing should feel easy to move through and easy to understand on a screen.

### Directness

The copy should reach the useful point without excessive introduction.

The customer should not have to pass through generic statements about "quality," "innovation," or "excellence" before learning what the product actually is or does.

### Simplicity

Use simple language when simple language communicates the product accurately.

Complexity should come from the product when necessary, not from the writer.

A customer should not need to reread a sentence just to understand its basic meaning.

### Arabic and Familiar Commerce Terms

Arabic remains the primary language.

Common non-Arabic commerce or technology terminology may be natural in some contexts when Saudi customers genuinely use it.

The goal is **familiarity**, not decoration.

The specific preferred and discouraged terms belong in `VOCABULARY.md`.

## Persuasion

### Helpful, Not Pushy

The copy should persuade by helping the customer understand the product, imagine its use, and recognize its value.

It should feel like useful selling rather than pressure.

### Benefits Without Hype

The default style is grounded.

The copy should not turn every product into:

> "أفضل منتج على الإطلاق"

or:

> "لا مثيل له"

merely because strong language sounds more promotional.

Strong claims belong only where supported and permitted by the project's rules.

### Customer-Centered Language

The writing should naturally address the customer's underlying questions:

> وش هو؟
> وش يفيدني فيه؟
> هل يناسب استخدامي؟
> ليش ممكن أختاره؟

The product should be described from the customer's perspective rather than as a database record.

---

## Saudi Cultural and Commercial Fit

The writing should feel appropriate for Saudi e-commerce and the expectations of Saudi online shoppers.

It should be locally natural without relying on stereotypes, forced dialect, exaggerated slang, or artificial cultural references.

Localness should come from **how the copy is written**, not from repeatedly announcing that it is Saudi.

---

## Desired Reading Experience

After reading the copy, the customer should generally feel:

> **"فهمت المنتج، عرفت وش يفيدني فيه، وأقدر أحدد إذا يناسبني."**

Depending on the product, the description may also create:

- الثقة;
- الراحة;
- الحماس;
- الرغبة;
- الاطمئنان;
- وضوح القيمة.

These are possible outcomes, not mandatory emotions for every product.

The customer should not feel that the copy is trying too hard to impress them.

---

## Style Boundaries

The desired voice sits inside these boundaries:

**Not formal MSA → not exaggerated dialect**

**Not corporate → not sloppy**

**Not cold → not overly familiar**

**Not passive → not pushy**

**Not generic → not artificially "Saudi"**

**Not boring → not constantly excited**

**Not literary → not simplistic**

These boundaries describe the character of the writing.

The detailed methods for achieving them belong in `RULES.md`, `VOCABULARY.md`, and `FORBIDDEN.md`.

## Style Examples

The examples below demonstrate character. They are **not templates to copy**.

### Desired

> "إذا تبي قهوة سهلة وسريعة التحضير، هذا الخيار مناسب لروتينك اليومي. حجمها عملي وتقدر تاخذها معك للدوام أو السفر."

The copy is direct, familiar, useful, and commercially natural.

### Too Formal

> "يُعد هذا المنتج خيارًا مناسبًا للأفراد الباحثين عن وسيلة عملية وفعالة تلبي متطلبات الاستخدام اليومي."

The meaning is understandable, but the language feels unnecessarily formal and corporate.

### Too Casual

> "هذا رهيب مرّة! صدق ما راح تندم إذا أخذته، وبيصير معك بكل مكان."

This may sound like personal conversation rather than professional store copy.

### Too Generic / AI-Like

> "يقدم هذا المنتج تجربة استثنائية تجمع بين الجودة العالية والأداء المتميز والتصميم العصري لتلبية مختلف احتياجاتك."

The language is broadly positive but says very little and could describe almost anything.

### Too Salesy

> "لا تفوت الفرصة! هذا المنتج لازم يكون عندك الآن، وأكيد راح يكون أفضل اختيار لك!"

The language applies pressure and certainty that are not justified by the product itself.

### Artificially "Saudi"

> "يا بعد حيي، هذا المنتج خرافي وبيخليك مبسوط مرّة!"

The sentence may contain Saudi-flavored expressions, but the localness is being performed rather than expressed through natural professional commerce.

The target is **Saudi-native commercial writing**, not maximum dialect density.
