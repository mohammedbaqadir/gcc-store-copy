# Template: Digital Product

**Type:** منتج رقمي — ebooks, courses, downloadable files, templates, digital documents, and other non-physical products delivered electronically.

## Purpose

Capture the information that tells the customer what digital value they receive, how they receive it, what it contains, how long they can access it, and what technical or usage constraints apply.

## High-value facts to supply

### Product identity and content

- Exact digital product name and version/edition when relevant.
- Product format: ebook, PDF, template, course, video, audio, files, etc.
- Main contents and included components.
- Page count, file count, module count, duration, or other useful size measures when supplied.
- Language(s).

### Access and delivery

- How the customer receives or accesses the product.
- Delivery timing: immediate, after review, or other stated timing.
- Access duration or expiry when applicable.
- Number of downloads or devices when restricted.
- Platform or account requirements.

### Compatibility and technical facts

- Supported file formats.
- Software/platform requirements.
- Device or operating-system compatibility when relevant.
- File size or other practical technical constraints when useful.

### Usage rights and updates

- License/use restrictions when supplied.
- Personal/commercial-use terms when supplied.
- Whether updates are included and how they are delivered.
- Versioning or edition differences.

### Course-specific facts

- Modules/topics.
- Total learning duration when supplied.
- Delivery format: live, recorded, self-paced, etc.
- Access period and completion conditions when applicable.
- Included materials or support.

## Prioritize first

When information is limited, prioritize: exact digital deliverable → what it contains → access/delivery method → format/platform requirements → access duration or usage rights when applicable.

## Customer questions this template should help answer

- What exactly do I get?
- What is inside it?
- How do I receive/access it?
- Can I use it on my device/software?
- How long do I have access?
- What am I allowed to do with it?
- What is included in the listed purchase?

## Copy implications

Make the digital deliverable concrete. Avoid vague promises such as "valuable content" when the actual contents can be described. For courses and educational products, describe coverage, format, and access rather than promising outcomes unless outcomes are explicitly supported.

## Do not infer

Do not infer lifetime access, instant delivery, compatibility, editable formats, resale rights, commercial rights, update entitlement, course certification, learning outcomes, or platform support unless supplied.
