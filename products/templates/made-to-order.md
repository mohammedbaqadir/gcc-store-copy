# Template: Made-to-Order Service

**Type:** خدمة حسب الطلب — custom or on-demand services such as design, printing, research, writing, and similar work.

## Purpose

Capture enough information to explain the service scope, what the customer receives, what the customer must provide, how customization works, and what constraints affect delivery or outcome.

## High-value facts to supply

### Service identity

- Exact service offered.
- What customer need or task the service addresses.
- Service variant, tier, format, or package when applicable.

### Scope and deliverables

- Exactly what is produced or performed.
- Number of items, pages, designs, outputs, sessions, or other measurable deliverables when applicable.
- File formats, physical formats, sizes, or print specifications.
- What is explicitly excluded.

### Customization and customer inputs

- Which parts can be customized.
- Information, files, measurements, references, or instructions the customer must provide.
- Required quality/format of supplied materials.
- Whether the customer chooses from fixed options or requests open-ended customization.

### Process and timing

- Main production steps when useful to the buying decision.
- Expected turnaround or delivery window when supplied.
- Revision rounds or correction policy when supplied.
- Approval points, proofing, or confirmation steps.

### Service constraints

- Eligibility or limitations.
- Supported topics, formats, quantities, sizes, languages, or channels.
- Capacity limits or dependencies.
- Shipping/collection details when the service produces a physical item.

### Commercially important terms

- What the listed price covers.
- Variables that can change the price when supplied.
- Included revisions, consultations, proofs, or other support.

## Prioritize first

When information is limited, prioritize: exact service → deliverable/scope → customer inputs required → turnaround → included revisions/limits → important exclusions.

## Customer questions this template should help answer

- What exactly will you do for me?
- What will I receive at the end?
- What do I need to send or prepare?
- How much can I customize it?
- How long does it take?
- How many revisions or changes are included?
- What is not included?

## Copy implications

For services, clarity of scope is usually more persuasive than exaggerated claims. Make the deliverable concrete. Explain the customer effort and process when that reduces uncertainty. State constraints plainly instead of hiding them behind promotional language.

## Do not infer

Do not infer professional qualifications, guaranteed results, originality guarantees, academic outcomes, turnaround promises, unlimited revisions, hidden inclusions, or expertise in a subject merely because the service category suggests them.

## Special caution for custom work

A service description must distinguish between what is guaranteed by the service definition and what depends on customer-provided materials, approvals, or requested changes.
