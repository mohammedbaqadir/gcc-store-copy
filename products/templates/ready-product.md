# Template: Ready Product

**Type:** المنتجات الجاهزة — tangible products that are stocked or otherwise ready to ship.

## Purpose

Capture the physical-product facts that let the copy explain what the customer is buying, what distinguishes the item, how it fits the intended use, and what will actually arrive.

## High-value facts to supply

### Identity and variant

- Product name and exact model/variant.
- Brand, line, or collection when relevant.
- Color, size, scent, flavor, finish, edition, capacity, or other variant selectors.
- SKU/model number when customer-facing or needed for identification.

### Physical characteristics

- Materials or composition when verified.
- Dimensions, weight, capacity, quantity, pack count, or included pieces.
- Technical specifications that materially affect use.
- Compatibility, supported devices, interfaces, or required accessories.
- Construction, finish, design characteristics, or distinctive physical features.

### What is included

- Exact contents of the package.
- Accessories, cables, cases, manuals, consumables, or bundled items.
- Whether an important pictured item is actually included.

### Use and customer fit

- Intended use or supported use cases.
- Relevant operating conditions or limitations.
- Who the product is for when that is factually defined.
- Important installation, setup, maintenance, or care information.

### Commercial/logistics facts

- Quantity sold per order.
- Stock/availability information when supplied for the task.
- Delivery or handling characteristics when part of the product proposition.
- Warranty or support terms only when supplied.

## Prioritize first

When information is limited, prioritize: exact product/variant → what is included → strongest distinguishing verified attributes → compatibility/use → important specifications or constraints.

## Customer questions this template should help answer

- What exactly is this?
- Which variant is this?
- What do I get in the box/order?
- Will it work with my setup?
- What are the important specifications?
- How would I actually use it?
- What limitation or compatibility issue should I know before buying?

## Copy implications

Lead with the identity and strongest verified differentiators. Translate useful specifications into concrete customer meaning where the connection is justified. Do not dump specifications merely because they exist.

## Do not infer

Do not infer material quality, durability, premium status, performance, origin, certification, safety, compatibility, waterproofing, health effects, warranty, included accessories, or suitability from appearance or category norms.

## Important omissions

A missing fact is not permission to use the typical fact for that product category. If a missing specification is essential to a customer decision, flag the gap rather than filling it from assumption.
