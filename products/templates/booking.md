# Template: Booking / Appointment Service

**Type:** حجوزات — scheduled services such as courses, consultations, medical appointments, appointments, and other time-bound bookings.

## Purpose

Capture the facts that remove booking uncertainty: what is being booked, with whom, when/how it happens, what is included, who can book, what the customer must prepare, and what scheduling rules apply.

## High-value facts to supply

### Service identity

- Exact service or appointment type.
- Provider, instructor, consultant, clinic, professional, or organization when customer-facing and supplied.
- Purpose or scope of the session/service.

### Timing and format

- Session/appointment duration.
- Available dates, times, or scheduling mechanism.
- Time zone when relevant.
- In-person, online, phone, or other format.
- Location or platform when supplied.
- Frequency or number of sessions for a multi-session booking.

### What is included

- What happens during the booking.
- Deliverables, follow-up, materials, assessment, or other included components.
- What is explicitly outside the booking scope.

### Eligibility and preparation

- Eligibility requirements.
- Age or participant requirements when supplied.
- Documents, information, equipment, or preparation the customer must provide.
- Prerequisites or prior knowledge when applicable.

### Scheduling and commercial terms

- Booking confirmation method.
- Rescheduling/cancellation rules when supplied.
- Late-arrival rules when supplied.
- Validity period for using a purchased booking when applicable.
- Number of attendees or participants allowed.
- What the price includes.

### Medical or sensitive-service facts

- Describe the service, provider, process, and stated scope accurately.
- Include medical/technical claims only when explicitly provided and appropriate to the product data.
- Do not turn a consultation or appointment description into a promise of diagnosis, treatment success, cure, or outcome.

## Prioritize first

When information is limited, prioritize: exact service → provider when supplied → duration and format → what is included → booking/eligibility requirements → scheduling constraints.

## Customer questions this template should help answer

- What exactly am I booking?
- Who provides it?
- How long does it take?
- Where/how does it happen?
- What is included?
- Do I need to prepare anything?
- Who is eligible?
- How do scheduling, cancellation, or rescheduling work?

## Copy implications

Booking copy should reduce uncertainty and make the next action obvious. Concrete logistics and scope matter more than generic service praise.

## Do not infer

Do not infer provider credentials, availability, eligibility, medical outcomes, guaranteed results, cancellation rights, rescheduling flexibility, duration, location, included follow-up, or platform support unless supplied.

## Critical distinction

A booking is a purchase of a defined service/time slot, not a guaranteed outcome. Describe the booked service precisely and keep outcomes grounded in verified information.
