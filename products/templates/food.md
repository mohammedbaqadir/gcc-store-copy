# Template: Food and Drink

**Type:** أكل — food and drinks requiring food-specific fulfillment or shipping/handling.

## Purpose

Capture the facts that let the copy describe what the customer will receive, its taste/format when supported, portion and contents, handling needs, and important dietary or storage information without guessing.

## High-value facts to supply

### Identity and contents

- Exact product name and variant.
- Ingredients or major components when supplied.
- Flavor, preparation style, filling, topping, sauce, seasoning, or other defining characteristics.
- Quantity, weight, serving size, piece count, bottle/cup size, or package size.

### Dietary and ingredient information

- Allergens and cross-contact information when supplied.
- Dietary attributes such as sugar-free, vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, etc. only when explicitly supported.
- Ingredient exclusions or substitutions that are actually offered.
- Halal or other certification/status only when verified and supplied.

### Freshness and handling

- Preparation method or whether the product is ready to eat, chilled, frozen, heated, or prepared after ordering.
- Shelf life or best-before information when supplied.
- Storage conditions.
- Reheating/preparation instructions where relevant.
- Packaging or temperature-control requirements when supplied.

### Fulfillment

- Pack configuration and what arrives in the order.
- Special shipping/handling conditions.
- Delivery timing constraints when part of the product information.
- Whether components are packed separately when relevant.

## Prioritize first

When information is limited, prioritize: exact item and contents → quantity/portion → key ingredient or dietary facts when supplied → storage/serving/handling → fulfillment constraints.

## Customer questions this template should help answer

- What exactly is in it?
- How much do I get?
- What does it taste like or contain, based on verified facts?
- Does it have an ingredient/allergen I need to know about?
- How is it stored or served?
- What will arrive and how will it be handled?

## Copy implications

Food copy should make the item easy to imagine without inventing sensory claims. Concrete ingredients, preparation details, portions, and serving/use information are stronger than generic praise.

## Do not infer

Do not infer taste intensity, freshness, health benefits, calories, allergen safety, shelf life, halal status, ingredient absence, preservation method, nutritional superiority, or temperature condition unless supported by the product data.

## Safety-critical gap

If an important ingredient, allergen, storage requirement, or handling condition is unknown, do not fill the gap from category knowledge. Keep the copy within known facts and flag the missing information when it matters to safe or informed purchase decisions.
