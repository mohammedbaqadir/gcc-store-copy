# Template: Digital Card

**Type:** بطاقة رقمية — recharge cards, account credit, activation codes, gift/value codes, and similar digital codes sold for electronic redemption.

## Purpose

Capture the exact value, target service/account, redemption conditions, delivery method, validity, and restrictions needed for a customer to know what code they are buying and whether they can use it.

## High-value facts to supply

### Identity and value

- Exact card/code type.
- Brand, platform, service, or account it applies to.
- Currency and face value/denomination.
- Region/country/storefront restrictions.
- Physical vs digital delivery, with digital treated as code-based delivery.

### Redemption

- Where and how the code is redeemed.
- Required account type or region.
- Activation requirements.
- Whether redemption is one-time or otherwise restricted.
- Any important redemption conditions.

### Validity and delivery

- Code validity/expiry when supplied.
- Delivery method and expected delivery timing.
- Whether the code is shown once, stored in an account, or sent by another stated method.
- Quantity of codes when more than one is sold.

### Restrictions and support

- Geographic restrictions.
- Platform/device restrictions.
- Region/account compatibility.
- Known exclusions or redemption limitations.
- What support can and cannot resolve when explicitly defined.

## Prioritize first

When information is limited, prioritize: target service/platform → denomination and currency → region/account compatibility → redemption method → delivery and validity.

## Customer questions this template should help answer

- What service/account is this for?
- How much value does it provide?
- Will it work with my account/region?
- How do I redeem it?
- When and how will I receive the code?
- Does it expire?
- Are there restrictions I should know before buying?

## Copy implications

For digital cards, compatibility and redemption clarity are often more valuable than promotional language. Put the target service, value, region, delivery, and redemption facts where customers can find them easily.

## Do not infer

Do not infer regional compatibility, guaranteed redemption, expiry, activation speed, refundability, account eligibility, or permanent validity from the brand or denomination alone.

## Critical rule

Never transform an unverified platform/region assumption into a compatibility claim. A code that looks correct may still have redemption restrictions that must come from the supplied product facts.
