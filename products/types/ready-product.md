# Ready Product — `ready_product`

Tangible products stocked or ready to ship.

**Facts that matter:** exact product and variant (color, size, capacity, model); brand or line when relevant; materials or composition when verified; dimensions, weight, capacity, pack count, contents of the package; compatibility, supported devices, required accessories; technical specs that affect use; intended use and operating limits; what is sold per order; delivery or warranty terms only when supplied.

**Do not infer:** material quality, durability, premium status, performance, origin, certification, safety, waterproofing, health effects, compatibility, included accessories, warranty, or suitability — from appearance or from category norms.

**Customer questions:** What exactly is it? Which variant? What is in the box? Will it work with my setup? What are the important specs? How would I use it? What limitation should I know before buying?

**Copy implications:** lead with identity and the strongest verified differentiators. Translate important specs into practical meaning where the connection is direct. Do not dump specs that exist only because they exist.
