# Digital Card — `digital_card`

Recharge cards, account credit, activation codes, and gift or value codes redeemed electronically.

**Facts that matter:** exact card or code type; brand, platform, service, or account it applies to; denomination and currency; region or storefront restrictions; delivery method (email, account, code display) and stated timing; redemption location and steps; required account type or region; activation requirements; one-time or limited redemption; validity or expiry when supplied; quantity of codes; support scope when explicitly defined; known exclusions.

**Do not infer:** regional compatibility, guaranteed redemption, expiry, activation speed, refundability, account eligibility, or permanent validity — from the brand or denomination alone.

**Customer questions:** What service or account is this for? How much value does it provide? Will it work with my account or region? How do I redeem it? When and how do I receive the code? Does it expire? What restrictions should I know before buying?

**Copy implications:** compatibility and redemption clarity are more persuasive than promotional language. Put target service, value, region, delivery, and redemption facts where customers find them easily. Never turn an unverified region assumption into a compatibility claim.
