# Made-to-Order Service — `made_to_order`

Custom or on-demand work: design, printing, research, writing, and similar services.

**Facts that matter:** exactly what is produced or performed; deliverable count (items, pages, outputs, sessions); file or physical formats; what can be customized and what the customer must supply; required quality or format of supplied materials; production steps; turnaround window; revision rounds and approval points; exclusions; what the price covers and what changes it; shipping or collection when the service produces a physical item.

**Do not infer:** professional qualifications, guaranteed results, originality guarantees, academic outcomes, turnaround promises, unlimited revisions, hidden inclusions, or expertise — from the service category.

**Customer questions:** What exactly will you do? What do I receive? What do I need to send? How much can I customize? How long does it take? How many revisions are included? What is not included?

**Copy implications:** scope clarity persuades more than praise. Make the deliverable concrete, state the customer's effort and process, and state constraints plainly instead of hiding them. Distinguish what the service guarantees from what depends on customer inputs or approvals.
