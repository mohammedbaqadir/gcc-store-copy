# Food and Drink — `food`

Food and beverages requiring food-specific fulfillment, handling, or shipping.

**Facts that matter:** exact item and variant; ingredients or major components when supplied; flavor, preparation style, filling, topping; quantity, weight, serving size, piece count, package size; allergens and cross-contact information when supplied; dietary attributes only when explicitly supported; halal or certification status only when verified and supplied; ready-to-eat / chilled / frozen / prepared-after-ordering; shelf life, storage, reheating; pack configuration; special shipping or handling conditions; delivery timing when part of the product information.

**Do not infer:** taste intensity, freshness, health benefits, calories, allergen safety, shelf life, halal status, ingredient absence, preservation method, nutritional superiority, or temperature condition.

**Customer questions:** What exactly is in it? How much do I get? What does it taste like based on verified facts? Is there an allergen I need to know about? How is it stored or served? What arrives and how is it handled?

**Copy implications:** make the item easy to imagine without inventing sensory claims. Concrete ingredients, preparation, portions, and serving context beat generic praise. If a safety-relevant fact is unknown, stay within known facts and surface the gap.
