# Booking — `booking`

Scheduled services: courses, consultations, appointments, and other time-bound offerings.

**Facts that matter:** exact service or appointment type; provider, instructor, consultant, or clinic when customer-facing and supplied; purpose and scope of the session; duration; available dates, times, or scheduling mechanism; time zone when relevant; in-person, online, or phone format; location or platform; number of sessions; what happens during the booking and what is included; what is explicitly outside scope; eligibility, age, or participant requirements; documents or preparation required; confirmation, rescheduling, cancellation, and late-arrival rules when supplied; validity period; attendees allowed; what the price includes.

**Do not infer:** provider credentials, availability, eligibility, medical outcomes, guaranteed results, cancellation rights, rescheduling flexibility, duration, location, follow-up, or platform support.

**Customer questions:** What exactly am I booking? Who provides it? How long does it take? Where or how does it happen? What is included? Do I need to prepare anything? Who is eligible? How do scheduling and cancellation work?

**Copy implications:** reduce uncertainty; make the next action obvious. Logistics and scope matter more than service praise. A booking is a defined service or time slot, not a promised outcome — describe it precisely and keep outcomes grounded in verified information.
