# Digital Product — `digital_product`

Ebooks, courses, downloadable files, templates, and other non-physical products delivered electronically.

**Facts that matter:** exact product name and version; format (ebook, PDF, template, course, video, audio); main contents and included components; page, file, or module count and duration when supplied; language; how the customer receives or accesses it; delivery timing as stated; access duration or expiry; download or device limits; platform or account requirements; file formats and software requirements; license and usage restrictions; whether updates are included.

**Do not infer:** lifetime access, instant delivery, compatibility, editable formats, resale or commercial rights, update entitlement, certification, learning outcomes, or platform support.

**Customer questions:** What exactly do I get? What is inside? How do I access it? Can I use it on my device or software? How long do I have access? What am I allowed to do with it?

**Copy implications:** make the deliverable concrete; avoid vague promises such as "valuable content" when the contents can be described. For courses and educational products, describe coverage, format, and access rather than promising outcomes. Describe digital delivery accurately, never with physical-shipping language.
